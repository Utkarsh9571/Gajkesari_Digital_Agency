# GAJKESARI — Digital Marketing Agency Website PRD

## 1️⃣ Overview
GAJKESARI is a digital marketing agency offering professional services and live project-based internship programs. The website aims to generate leads from businesses seeking marketing services and students looking for internship/training opportunities.

**Project Type:** Public-facing website  
**Tech Stack:** Next.js (React), Node.js, MongoDB, Tailwind CSS  
**Branding:** Logo available  
**Deployment:** SEO-focused production build

---

## 2️⃣ Goals
- Showcase GAJKESARI's expertise and services
- Generate business leads via contact + WhatsApp CTAs
- Attract internship applicants and provide detailed program information
- Establish branding and credibility
- SEO optimized to rank for digital marketing services

---

## 3️⃣ Target Audience
1. Business owners looking for digital marketing solutions
2. Students & professionals seeking real-world digital marketing training

---

## 4️⃣ Key Features (v1 Release)
| Feature | Description |
|--------|-------------|
| Responsive UI | Works across desktop, mobile, tablet |
| Lead Forms | Forms for Services & Internships |
| Contact Channels | Form, WhatsApp button, Phone, Email |
